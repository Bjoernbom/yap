Push-to-talk dictation and meeting notes for the Mac. Hold a key, say what you mean, let go. It all runs on your Mac. Nothing leaves it.

Free and open source · macOS 26+ · Apple silicon

two things, done well

No settings maze. Good defaults and one screen of settings.

Dictate

Hold fn, talk, let go. The text lands where your cursor is.

  • Transcribes while you talk, so a long ramble pastes as fast as a short one.
  • Picks up 25 European languages on its own.
  • Style follows the app: casual in Slack, proper in Mail, identifiers intact in Xcode.
  • Double-tap to go hands-free. Esc cancels. ⌃⌘V pastes the last one again.

Notes

Record a meeting with ⌥⌘N. Get a clean note.

  • You and the call as two tracks, split into speakers.
  • Title, summary, decisions and action items, plus the full transcript.
  • Plain Markdown files in a folder you choose.
  • Notices Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack and FaceTime calls. No bot joins your meeting.

runs on your Mac. nothing leaves it.

Speech and language models run on-device. The only network calls are the one-time model download and update checks. No telemetry, no accounts, no API keys.

install

Paste this into Terminal. It downloads the newest yap, checks it and moves it to Applications.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Bjoernbom/yap/main/scripts/install.sh | sh

Homebrew

brew install --cask bjoernbom/tap/yap

Download

Get the DMG from Releases. yap isn't notarized, so the first launch is blocked once: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Needs macOS 26 or later, an Apple silicon Mac and about 1 GB free for the speech model. Polish and summaries use Apple Intelligence when it's on.
